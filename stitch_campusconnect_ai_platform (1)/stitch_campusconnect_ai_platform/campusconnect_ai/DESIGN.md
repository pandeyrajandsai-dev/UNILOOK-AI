## Brand & Style

This design system drives a premium, forward-leaning AI academic portal engineered for prospective and enrolled students exploring institutional resources, admissions data, and campus services. The aesthetic fuses **Atmospheric Technical Minimalism** with **Refined Academic Sci-Fi**, borrowing the poise of elite university archives and coupling it with the kinetic immediacy of modern autonomous systems.

### Target Audience & Core Experience
The interface caters to ambitious scholars, international applicants, and academic advisors. The user experience prioritizes low cognitive friction, cinematic focus, and authoritative clarity:
- **Autonomous & Attentive:** Interfaces prioritize proactive conversational UI, crisp data structures, and instant, non-judgmental query resolution.
- **Academic Rigor meets Machine Precision:** High typographic discipline balances atmospheric luminescence. The platform projects institutional prestige without institutional stiffness.
- **Visual Stance:** Dark, deep layers of carbon and graphite offset by hyper-focused luminescent emerald accents. The UI feels calibrated, private, and nocturnal—mirroring the focused energy of late-night research and critical decisions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, nocturnal canvas punctuated by organic greens, cybernetic luminescence, and surgical alerts.

### Roles and Application

- **Primary Background (`#111315` - Charcoal Black):** The deepest foundation. Used for the overall canvas and viewports. Sets zero-glare, OLED-depth immersion.
- **Secondary Background (`#191D1B` - Deep Charcoal):** Structural foundation for sidebars, persistent panels, canvas wells, and structural containers.
- **Surface / Card Background (`#202522` - Dark Graphite):** Elevated surface token for modules, interactive query containers, conversational bubbles, and modal sheets.
- **Primary Accent (`#1F6B45` - Forest Green):** The institutional anchor. Applied to primary interactive controls, confirmed statuses, active navigation items, and prominent branded focal points.
- **Secondary Accent (`#174C36` - Deep Emerald):** Supportive depth token. Applied to button hover states, layered card outlines, and ambient gradient backings.
- **Neon Accent (`#7CFF9B` - Subtle Neon Green):** Represents active AI computation, stream indicators, live assistant presence, focus states, and precision micro-accents. Used sparingly to avoid visual fatigue.
- **Highlight Accent (`#DC3545` - Crimson Red):** Reserved exclusively for admissions deadlines, critical validation alerts, systemic errors, and destructive operations.
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - *Primary (`#F3F5F3` - Soft White):* 96% luminance with a gentle cool-sage bias to minimize harsh contrast fatigue.
  - *Secondary (`#A4ACA6` - Muted Grey):* Supporting metadata, input placeholders, timestamps, and academic citations.
- **Stroke & Structural Boundaries (`#2A322D` - Subtle Dark Grey):** Precision boundary lines, micro-dividers, and inset outlines with calculated forest-green/neon ambient proximity glows.

## Typography

The typography couples the modern warmth and geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural elements and dynamic headings with the neutral clarity of **Inter** for sustained reading, tabular admissions reports, and conversational AI outputs.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans. Employs tight negative letter-spacing for large scales to generate an authoritative, modern voice. Never use pure `#FFFFFF`; all headlines utilize `#F3F5F3` to prevent ocular strain across dark modules.
- **Body & Dialogue:** Rendered in Inter. Generous line-height (`1.5` to `1.6`) ensures frictionless consumption of multi-paragraph AI advice, faculty profiles, and departmental prerequisites.
- **Labels, Badges & AI Metas:** Micro-copy, status chips, and categorization tags use Plus Jakarta Sans at uppercase or bold title case with slightly expanded tracking to maintain legibility against dark slate surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The system leverages a strict 8-point base spatial model within a fluid grid framework designed to handle both data-dense query dashboards and cinematic AI conversational flows.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid. Outer margins are `margin-lg` (48px) with `gutter-lg` (32px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1440px` for optimal eye scanning across institutional intelligence modules.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid. Outer margins are `margin` (32px) with `gutter` (24px). Split panels stack gracefully into vertical segments.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin-sm` (16px) and `gutter-sm` (16px). Persistent floating action dock for rapid query inputs with bottom-safe offsets.

### Structural Flow
Conversational interfaces allocate 65% of the viewport width on desktop to the core AI reasoning and transcript view, using the remaining 35% for dynamic sidecars (course outlines, admission timelines, credential checklists).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque dropshadows. Instead, depth is articulated through **Luminescent Tonal Stratification** and **Backdrop Diffusion**.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#111315`. Ground level for main application viewports.
- **Tier 1 (Structural Insets & Wells):** Background `#191D1B` with 1px border `#2A322D`. Used for conversational view channels, filter backdrops, and navigation panels.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Cards):** Background `#202522`. Border 1px `#2A322D`. Shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. Used for course modules, query responses, and search filters.
- **Tier 3 (Glassmorphic Translucent Headers & Sheets):** Background `rgba(32, 37, 34, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`. Border 1px `rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 4 (Active AI State / Luminescent Focus):** Border transitions to `rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.45)` accompanied by an ambient bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(31, 107, 69, 0.35), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a tuned curvature profile that balances tech ergonomics with academic authority.

### Radius Tokens
- **Standard Controls & Elements (`rounded` - 8px / 0.5rem):** Buttons, textual input fields, nested tooltips, and utility icon wrappers.
- **Cards & Content Modules (`rounded-lg` - 16px / 1rem):** Institutional comparison cards, AI dialog messages, faculty summaries, and search overlays.
- **Floating Modals & Primary Containers (`rounded-xl` - 24px / 1.5rem):** Main hero search portals, admission roadmap panels, and persistent bottom interactive trays.
- **Pills / Circular Elements (`rounded-full`):** Status indicator points, active AI computation badges, filter chips, and user avatars.

- **Secondary Action (Subtle Dark):** Background `#202522`, text `#F3F5F3`, border 1px solid `#2A322D`. On hover: background `#2A322D`, border-color `rgba(243, 245, 243, 0.25)`.
- **Ghost / Minimal:** Background transparent, text `#A4ACA6`. On hover: text `#7CFF9B`, background `rgba(31, 107, 69, 0.15)`.

### Badges & Chips
- **AI Status Badge:** Background `rgba(31, 107, 69, 0.2)`, text `#7CFF9B`, border 1px solid `rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.3)`. Includes a pulsating 6px neon dot representing live AI processing.
- **Deadline / Priority Badge:** Background `rgba(220, 53, 69, 0.15)`, text `#FF6B7A`, border 1px solid `rgba(220, 53, 69, 0.4)`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Autonomous Prompt Bar:** Container height 56px, background `rgba(25, 29, 27, 0.85)` with backdrop blur, border 1px solid `#2A322D`, radius 16px (`rounded-lg`). Left-side AI icon in `#7CFF9B`. Typography uses `body-lg` in `#F3F5F3`. Focus state ignites a subtle outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.3)`.
- **Standard Field:** Background `#191D1B`, border 1px solid `#2A322D`, text `#F3F5F3`, placeholder `#A4ACA6`. Focus shifts border to `#1F6B45`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Academic Program Card:** Built on `#202522`, padded with `space-lg` (24px), radius 16px (`rounded-lg`), hairline border `#2A322D`. Hovering elevates the card with a top highlight border `1px solid rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.4)` and a translateY of `-2px`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Selectors:** 18px dimensions, background `#191D1B`, border 1px solid `#2A322D`, radius 4px (checkbox) or circular (radio). When checked: fill `#1F6B45` with `#7CFF9B` check mark or center dot, accompanied by an ambient `0 0 8px rgba(124, 255, 155, 0.4)` bloom.

### Specialized AI & Academic Components
- **Streaming Response Card:** Surface `#202522` with a dynamic left-border stripe in `#7CFF9B`. Generates inline citation tokens that link directly to authoritative syllabus, fee, or faculty documentation.
- **Criteria Matrix Grid:** High-density comparison table with `#191D1B` column headers, alternating row striping with transparent `#202522`, and `#2A322D` grid lines.
